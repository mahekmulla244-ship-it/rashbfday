Sirf aapke liye. ❤️

Meri Dil Ki Baat Padho ❤️

Aap Mere Liye Itne Special Kyun Ho? ❤️

Shayad main har baar sahi tareeke se bata nahi paayi, lekin aap mere liye sirf ek relationship ka naam nahi ho. Aap meri zindagi ka woh hissa ho jiske saath judi hui bahut saari feelings hain.

🫶

Aapka Hona

Kabhi kabhi kisi insaan ka bas hona hi bahut hota hai. Aapka message, aapki awaaz, aapka care karna, aapka gussa, aapki choti choti baatein... Sab kuch mere liye important raha hai.

🥺

Aapki Importance

Aapki importance mujhe tab aur zyada samajh aayi jab cheezein waise nahi rahi jaise pehle hua karti thi. Tab samajh aaya ki kuch log aadat nahi hote... Woh dil ka ek important hissa ban jaate hain.

❤️

Meri Feelings

Meri feelings sirf isliye khatam nahi ho gayi kyunki humare beech difficult time aaya. Main aaj bhi aapko dil se care karti hoon, aapki respect karti hoon aur aapki khushi mere liye matter karti hai.

🌷

Aap Mere Liye Khaas Ho

Har relationship perfect nahi hota. Lekin kuch relationships humein khud ko samajhna sikha dete hain. Aapne mujhe mere baare mein bahut kuch samjhaya hai. Aur isliye aap mere liye hamesha special rahoge.

Woh Choti Choti Baatein... 💗

Kabhi kabhi pyaar bade gestures mein nahi, balki un choti choti cheezon mein hota hai jo hum bina notice kiye yaad rakh lete hain.

💬

Aapke Messages

Aapka ek simple sa message bhi kabhi kabhi pura mood change kar deta tha. Chahe message chota ho, lekin mere liye uske peeche aapka time aur effort important tha.

😂

Humari Bakchodi

Humari random baatein, random jokes, bina matlab ki conversations, aur woh hasi... Yeh sab memories mere liye bahut precious hain.

🥹

Woh Care

Aapne jis tarah meri care ki, meri choti choti baaton ko notice kiya, aur mujhe important feel karaya... Us sabki value mujhe ab aur zyada samajh aati hai.

Mujhe Bahut Kuch Samajh Aaya... 🥺

Main maanta hoon ki mujhse bahut galtiyan hui hain. Main immature thi. Aap khud apni problems se deal kar rahe the, lekin main us waqt aapki situation ko utna samajh nahi paayi. Mujhe lagta raha ki mujhe aapka time chahiye, aapka attention chahiye, aap mere saath zyada time spend karo... Aur jab mujhe woh nahi milta tha, toh main baar baar fight karne lagti thi. Mujhe ab samajh aata hai ki pyaar ka matlab sirf kisi se time maangna nahi hota. Kabhi kabhi pyaar ka matlab hota hai samne wale ki situation samajhna, uski problems ko space dena, aur uske saath khade rehna bina har cheez ko apne against liye. Us waqt main yeh nahi samajh paayi. Lekin ab main samajhna chahti hoon. Aur sirf samajhna nahi... Main khud ko improve bhi karna chahti hoon.

Humari Kahani Ne Mujhe Kya Sikhaya ❤️

🌸 Jab Sab Kuch Naya Tha

Jab hum pehli baar mile the, tab mujhe nahi pata tha ki ek online game se shuru hui ek simple si baat itni important ban jayegi. Dheere dheere baatein badhi, connection bana, aur phir aap meri life ka ek beautiful part ban gaye.

💕 Jab Hum Close Hue

Humari conversations, hasi-mazaak, care, late conversations, choti choti nok-jhok... Sab kuch milkar ek alag hi duniya ban gayi thi. Aur shayad isi liye aap mere liye sirf "koi" nahi rahe.

🥺 Jab Mushkilein Aayi

Phir ek point aaya jab hum dono ke beech cheezein difficult ho gayi. Main apni feelings ko handle nahi kar paayi. Main baar baar time aur attention maangti rahi, fights karti rahi, aur aapki situation ko properly samajh nahi paayi.

🌱 Ab Mujhe Kya Samajh Aaya

Ab mujhe pata hai ki relationship sirf pyaar feel karne ka naam nahi hai. Relationship mein understanding, patience, respect, space, trust aur maturity bhi chahiye. Aur main in sab cheezon par khud ke upar kaam karna chahti hoon.

Aapne Mujhe Jo 3 Mahine Diye... 🥺❤️

Main in teen mahino ko sirf ek "chance" ki tarah nahi dekhna chahti. Main ise ek opportunity samajhti hoon... Khud ko better banane ki. Khud ko samajhne ki. Aur apni galtiyon ko repeat na karne ki.

Aapne mujhe 3 months diye. Aur mere liye yeh 3 months sirf isliye important nahi hain kyunki mujhe aapko prove karna hai. Yeh mere liye important hain kyunki mujhe khud ko bhi prove karna hai ki main change kar sakti hoon. Main apni immaturity ko sirf words mein nahi, apni actions mein change karna chahti hoon. Main har baar attention maangne ke bajaye understanding seekhna chahti hoon. Har choti baat par fight karne ke bajaye pehle calmly sochna chahti hoon. Aapki situation ko samajhna chahti hoon. Aapki problems ko seriously lena chahti hoon. Aur sabse important... Main chahti hoon ki aapko mere saath pressure nahi, peace feel ho.

Mere Aap Se Chote Chote Vaade 💗

Main bade bade promises karke sirf words nahi bolna chahti. Main choti choti cheezon mein apna change dikhana chahti hoon.

  • Main har baat ko fight mein convert karne ke bajaye pehle samajhne ki koshish karungi.
  • Main aapki busy life ya problems ko personal rejection nahi samjhungi.
  • Main aapko unnecessarily pressure nahi dungi.
  • Main apni feelings ko better tareeke se express karna seekhungi.
  • Main aapko sirf apni expectations se nahi, aapki feelings se bhi samajhne ki koshish karungi.
  • Main apni maturity par genuinely kaam karungi.
  • Main apni health aur fitness par bhi properly focus karungi.
  • Main sirf bolungi nahi... apne actions se change dikhane ki koshish karungi.
  • Aur sabse important, main aapko ek insaan ki tarah samajhne ki koshish karungi, sirf apne boyfriend ki tarah nahi.

Kuch Baatein Jo Main Kabhi Sahi Se Keh Nahi Paayi... 🥺

Mere Jaan, ❤️

Shayad main har baar apni feelings sahi words mein express nahi kar paati. Kabhi main zyada emotional ho jaati hoon, kabhi zyada expect kar leti hoon, kabhi gusse mein woh bol deti hoon jo actually mere dil mein hota hi nahi. Lekin ek baat sach hai... Aap mere liye bahut important ho.

Mujhe pata hai ki sirf "I love you" bol dene se sab kuch theek nahi hota. Isliye main aapse sirf words mein kuch prove nahi karna chahti. Main chahti hoon ki time ke saath aap khud notice karo ki Mahek pehle wali Mahek nahi rahi.

Main perfect banne ka promise nahi karungi. Kyuki main perfect nahi hoon. Main bas itna promise kar sakti hoon ki main apni mistakes ko ignore nahi karungi. Main seekhungi. Main grow karungi. Main apne emotions ko better handle karungi. Aur main apne aap ko ek better version banane ki genuinely koshish karungi.

Aapne jo bhi problems face ki, shayad main us waqt unka proper part nahi samajh paayi. Lekin ab main samajhna chahti hoon. Aapko sunna chahti hoon. Aapko samajhna chahti hoon. Aur agar kabhi meri wajah se aapko hurt hua, toh main us feeling ko lightly nahi lena chahti.

Bas itna chahti hoon... Aap mujhe mere words se nahi, mere efforts se dekho. ❤️

Main Aaj Bhi Aapki Itni Care Kyun Karti Hoon? ❤️

❤️

Kyunki Aap Mere Liye Important Ho

Simple. Aap important ho. Aapki happiness important hai. Aapki problems important hain. Aapka peace important hai. Aur main chahti hoon ki main bhi aapke liye ek peaceful person ban paun.

🌷

Kyunki Memories Important Hain

Humne jo time saath spend kiya, jo conversations hui, jo hasi, jo moments... Woh sab mere liye fake nahi the. Woh mere liye real the. Aur isliye woh memories aaj bhi mere dil ke bahut close hain.

🥹

Kyunki Main Give Up Nahi Karna Chahti

Main kisi cheez ko force nahi karna chahti. Bas apni taraf se genuinely better banna chahti hoon. Agar feelings sach hain, toh unke saath responsibility bhi honi chahiye. Aur main woh responsibility samajhna chahti hoon.

Happy Boyfriend's Day, Mere Jaan ❤️

Aaj Boyfriend's Day par main aapko koi expensive gift nahi de rahi. Bas apne dil ka ek chhota sa part aapke saamne rakh rahi hoon.

Aap mere liye woh insaan ho jiske saath maine sirf happy moments nahi, balki khud ko samajhna bhi seekha. Aapke saath rehkar mujhe pyaar ka ek alag meaning samajh aaya. Pyaar sirf kisi ko paana nahi hota. Pyaar ka matlab hota hai samajhna, respect karna, grow karna, aur jab zarurat ho khud ko improve karna.

Is Boyfriend's Day par meri bas ek wish hai... Ki aap hamesha khush raho. Aapki life mein peace ho. Aap apne goals achieve karo. Aur jo bhi problems aap face kar rahe ho, un sabko handle karne ki strength mile.

Aur agar meri life mein kabhi mujhe aapka saath phir se properly milta hai... Toh main chahti hoon ki iss baar main uski value aur zyada samjhu.

❤️

Meri Kahani Ka Yeh End Nahi Hai...

Main yeh page kisi pressure ke liye nahi bana rahi. Na hi main chahti hoon ki aap sirf meri feelings dekhkar koi decision lo. Main bas chahti hoon ki aap ek baar meri woh side dekho jo shayad main pehle properly dikha nahi paayi.


Main immature thi. Mujhse mistakes hui. Main aapki situation nahi samajh paayi. Main zyada attention chahti rahi. Fights hui. Things complicated hui. Lekin in sab ke beech ek cheez genuine thi... Aapke liye meri feelings. ❤️


Aur ab mere paas sirf words nahi hain. Mere paas 3 months hain. 3 months khud ko better banane ke. 3 months apni maturity par kaam karne ke. 3 months apni health aur fitness par focus karne ke. 3 months yeh samajhne ke ki ek healthy relationship ko actually kya chahiye.


Main nahi jaanti future mein exactly kya hoga. Lekin main itna zaroor jaanti hoon... Main apni taraf se genuinely better banne ki koshish karungi. Aur agar kabhi aap meri taraf dekho, toh main chahti hoon ki aapko ek aisi Mahek dikhe jo aapko sirf pyaar nahi karti... balki aapko samajhna bhi seekh gayi hai.
